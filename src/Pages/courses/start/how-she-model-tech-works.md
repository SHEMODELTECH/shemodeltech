# Start Here: How She Model Tech Works

Time: about 15 minutes

New to She Model Tech? Learn how the platform works: find your way around, set up your profile, join a project or lead one, earn badges, and get seen by companies.

It takes about 15 minutes. Work through the sections in order, and keep She Model Tech open in another tab so you can try each step as you read.

---

## Section 1: Find Your Way Around

**Goal:** Know where everything lives, so you never feel lost.

When you sign in, you land on **Home**, your dashboard. The top of Home shows a **Getting started** checklist with your next steps. Each step has a **Show me** button that takes you to the right page, and it ticks itself off when you finish it.

The menu on the left (on a phone, tap the menu icon) has everything else:

- **Home**: your dashboard, with your projects, courses, and badges in one place.
- **Projects**: **All Projects** to find a project to join, **My Projects** for the projects you're part of, and **Project Vault** for finished projects.
- **Workspace**: the team space for each project you're on.
- **Proof Wall**: what members are building, and where you share your own progress.
- **Jobs**: roles posted by verified companies.
- **Events**: online workshops, tech talks, podcasts, and demo days.
- **Talent Board**: where companies find members with verified badges.
- **Support**: answers to common questions, and a form to contact us.
- **Settings**: your profile, email settings, and payout details.

At the top of the page you'll find **Learning** (all the free courses), your **messages**, and your **notifications**.

## Section 2: Set Up Your Profile

**Goal:** Complete your profile, so project leads and companies can see who you are.

Go to **Settings**, then **Profile**. Your profile is your first impression on She Model Tech: leads read it when you apply to a project, and companies read it on the Talent Board.

1. **Choose your skill track.** This is the area you want to grow in, such as Coding Developer, Quality Tester, or Product / Project Owner. You can still join projects in other tracks.
2. **Set your experience level.** Be honest. Every level is welcome, and teams mix people at different levels on purpose.
3. **Add your LinkedIn profile.** Your profile counts as complete once your track, experience level, and LinkedIn are in.
4. **Add a photo, and a portfolio or GitHub link** if you have one. They help leads choose you.

Two more settings are worth a minute:

- **Email visibility**: choose whether other members can see your email. They can always message you on She Model Tech.
- **Email settings**: choose how often we email you (every weekday, or three times a week), or turn emails off.

## Section 3: Learn With Free Courses

**Goal:** Use She Model Tech Learning to build skills before and during a project.

Open **Learning** at the top of the page. Every course is free, across six tech tracks, from your first program to AI engineering, product, security, and leadership.

- **Start a course** by opening it and tapping start. Your progress is saved to your account, so you can stop and pick up where you left off, on any device.
- **Quizzes and checklists** inside each course help you check your understanding.
- **Finish a course** to get a certificate of completion you can download and add to LinkedIn. To finish, write a short summary (30+ words) for each module, pass every quiz with 70% or more, reply to another learner in the course forum (once others have posted), and add your name. This Start Here guide has no certificate.
- **My learning** shows the courses you're taking and the ones you've finished.

Courses build the skill. Badges come from doing the work on a real project, which is what the next sections cover.

## Section 4: Find and Join a Project

**Goal:** Know how to choose a project and apply to join a team.

Go to **Projects**, then **All Projects**. Each project card shows a label that tells you where it is:

- **Needs a Lead**: the project is waiting for someone to lead it.
- **Being set up**: a lead has been chosen and is preparing the project.
- **Open to Join**: the team is open, and you can apply for a role now.
- **Closed**: the team is full.

Members 13 and older can join free projects. Tap **Join the Project** on a project card (or anywhere on the card) to open the project's page. What you can do there depends on its label.

**On a project that is open to join**, tap **Apply to This Project** on its page. A short application form opens, where you:

1. Choose the **role** you want on the team.
2. Choose the **badge track** this project should count toward.
3. Share your skills, why you're interested, a portfolio or résumé link, and your LinkedIn.

The project lead reviews applications. When you're approved, you'll get a notification and the project appears in your **Workspace**. Every role is open to every level. Roles marked **Good for beginners** are a great start; roles marked **Stretch role** are best with some experience, but you can still apply and challenge yourself.

**On a project that needs a lead**, tap **Join** on its page, then choose how to join:

- **Lead this project**: apply to lead it (see Section 5).
- **Join the team**: be a collaborator. We'll notify you as soon as the lead opens roles, so you can apply first.

**On a project that is being set up**, the card shows **Ongoing application** instead of Join the Project. Open it and tap **Join** to go on the list, and we'll notify you as soon as the lead opens roles.

**Paid projects** are run and paid for by She Model Tech, for members 18 and older who have earned at least one badge. The pay for each role is shown before you apply, and you're paid by PayPal or Wise after the work is reviewed. Add your payout email in **Settings**, **Account**. Paid projects don't award badges, so free projects are where you earn them.

## Section 5: Lead a Project

**Goal:** Understand how to apply to lead, and what a lead does.

You don't need badges or past experience to lead. Leading is a skill-building path of its own.

**To apply**, find a project labelled **Needs a Lead**, tap **Join the Project** to open it, tap **Join**, and choose **Lead this project**. You'll:

1. **Rank up to three projects** in order. If someone else leads your first choice, we look at your second and third.
2. Say in a couple of sentences **why you want to lead**.
3. Share the **hours a week** you can give.

She Model Tech reviews every lead application and may invite you to a short chat first, because a team depends on its lead. If no project needs a lead right now, join the **waitlist** and we'll email you when one does.

**What a lead does:**

- **Sets up the project** in the workspace **Resources** tab: a submission link where the work lives (a GitHub repository is recommended), a meeting link for team calls, and a project details document.
- **Opens roles and reviews applications** to build the team.
- **Runs the team** in the **Discussion** tab: plans the work, keeps everyone moving, and answers questions.
- **Submits the project for review** when the work is done, then confirms the badges for the team once it's approved.

Leads earn a TechPO (Product / Project Owner) badge when the project is completed, plus a certificate documenting the project. Teams of 7 or more also have a **Product Co-owner** seat, which earns TechPO too.

## Section 7: Earn Badges and Level Up

**Goal:** Understand how badges are earned and how levels grow.

A badge is verified proof of real work. Here's how it happens:

1. When you apply to a free project, you choose the **badge track** it counts toward.
2. When the work is done, the lead submits the project for review.
3. She Model Tech reviews it and either **approves** it, **asks for changes** (the team improves the work and submits again), or **rejects** it.
4. Once it's approved, the lead confirms each member's badge, and you earn a badge in your track.

There are six badge tracks: TechDev (Coding Developer), TechArchs (Low/No-Code Developer), TechQA (Quality Tester), TechGuard (Network & Cybersecurity), TechPO (Product / Project Owner), and TechLeads (Non-Technical Roles).

Your **level** in a track grows with the number of badges you hold in it:

- **Novice**: 1 to 2 badges
- **Associate**: 3 to 6 badges
- **Advanced**: 7 to 11 badges
- **Expert**: 12 or more badges

#### Quiz
1. When do you choose which track a project's badge counts toward?
2. What happens if She Model Tech asks for changes to a project?
3. How many badges in a track do you need to reach Associate?

*Answers: 1) When you apply to the project. 2) The team improves the work and submits it again. 3) Three.*
